# ShieldSafe Interactive Demonstration Script

## Act I: Onboarding & Profile Customization
1. Navigate to `/`.
2. Click "Get Started" to enter `/onboarding`.
3. Review the three profiles (Everyday Safety, Women's Safety, Child & Teen Safety).
4. Select "Women's Safety" and click "Confirm & Open Dashboard".

## Act II: Digital Harassment Defender
1. From the dashboard, click "Analyze a Message" to enter `/analyze`.
2. Click on the demo sample button "Threatening message (urgent)".
3. Observe the message text populated and click "Analyze Message Patterns".
4. Review the detected flags (Direct Threat, Location Surveillance) and practical guidance.
5. Click "Save as Incident Record". The form at `/incidents/new` opens prefilled with the evidence quotes.
6. Submit the incident.
7. Add a follow-up action note: "Reported to local cybercrime helpline ref #2841."
8. Click "Export PDF Report" to download a clean, structured incident summary locally.

## Act III: Local Safety Map & Community Verification
1. Click "Safety Map" in the sidebar (`/map`).
2. Observe seeded hazards with colored markers on the OpenStreetMap.
3. Toggle between Map View and List View.
4. Click "Report Hazard" (`/report-hazard`), fill in a broken streetlight report, and submit.
5. Verify the new report appears on the map and confirm a hazard report with the "Confirm Hazard" button.

## Act IV: Journey Guardian & Share Link
1. Click "Journey Guardian" in the sidebar (`/journey`).
2. Enter destination "Metro Station" and start point "College Campus". Set 15-minute check-in interval.
3. Click "Start Journey Guardian Session".
4. Observe the active countdown timer and click "Generate Share Link".
5. Copy the generated URL and open it in a private browser tab to verify the contact view.
6. Back in the main app, click "Revoke Share Link" and confirm the public view is immediately invalidated.
7. Click "Arrived Safely (Complete)".
